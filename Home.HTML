Welcome to the Cal Poly Nuclear Disarmament Club

Our Mission:

The Nuclear Disarmament Club is dedicated to promoting global peace and security through the reduction and eventual elimination of nuclear weapons. We seek to educate our community about the dangers of nuclear proliferation, advocate for disarmament policies at local, national, and international levels, and empower individuals to take meaningful action for a nuclear-free world. Through awareness campaigns, dialogue, and partnerships, we strive to build a future rooted in diplomacy, mutual understanding, and sustainable peace.

Our Goals:

1. Actively pursue multilateral and verifiable nuclear disarmament with the Nuclear Weapon States.

2. Renounce the first use of nuclear weapons.

3. End the sole and unchecked authority of a U.S. president to launch nuclear weapons.

4. Remove all U.S. nuclear weapons from "hair-trigger alert.".
